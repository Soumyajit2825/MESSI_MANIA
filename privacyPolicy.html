Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 05, 2025

Welcome to MessiMania! We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your experience on our platform is secure. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you visit www.messimania.com (the "Site").

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Data: Name, email address, and other details you provide when signing up for newsletters or creating an account.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with the Site, such as IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent.
  • Cookies: Small files stored on your device to enhance your experience (e.g., remembering preferences). You can manage cookie settings in your browser.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data helps us:

  • Provide and improve MessiMania’s content and services.
  • Send you updates, promotions, or Messi-related news (with your consent).
  • Analyze Site usage to optimize performance and user experience.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

3. How We Protect Your Data

We take your privacy seriously and use:

  • Encryption to secure data transmission.
  • Secure servers to store your information.
  • Regular security updates to prevent unauthorized access.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with:

  • Trusted service providers (e.g., email or hosting services) under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal authorities if required by law.

5. Your Rights

Under applicable laws (e.g., GDPR for EU users), you can:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data.
  • Opt out of marketing emails anytime via the unsubscribe link.
  • Request details about how your data is processed.

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@messimania.com.

6. Third-Party Links

MessiMania may link to external sites (e.g., social media, sponsors). We’re not responsible for their privacy practices—please review their policies.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as needed. Changes will be posted here with the updated date.

8. Contact Us

Questions? Reach out at privacy@messimania.com.
